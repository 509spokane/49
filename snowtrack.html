Accuracy by days out

Each forecast's predicted snow for a day is compared with 49°'s morning 24-hour new-snow report. Avg miss is the typical error in inches. Bias shows whether a forecast tends to call too much (+) or too little (−). Snow calls counts how often it correctly said whether at least 1" would fall.
